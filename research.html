Speculating on Risks of AI Clones to Selfhood and Relationships: Doppelganger-phobia, Identity Fragmentation, and Living Memories

PYK Lee, NF Ma, IJ Kim, D Yoon · CSCW 2023

My first research publication ever. I couldn't sleep at all the night before I first submitted this to CHI '22 because I was so nervous about how it would turn out. When it was rejected I seriously had to sit down and ask myself whether I could see myself pursuing a career where rejection after working on something for months would happen more often than not. Ultimately a few months later we revised the paper for CSCW '23 where it was accepted. I deal with paper rejections a lot better these days!

I'm particularly indebted on this project to Dongwook and Ning, who not only showed me the ropes of conducting empirical HCI work, but also believed very strongly in my potential as an undergraduate researcher even when I didn't.

It's also interesting for me to think back on the fact that no participant in this research project even knew what an LLM was at the time this research was conducted (Summer 2021). A great source of low-background data on attitudes towards technology that would come to dominate digital life just a year later.

Negotiating Relationships with ChatGPT: Perceptions, External Influences, and Strategies for AI Companionship

PYK Lee, JY Bo, Z Zhao, PA Aoyagui, M Varona, A Anderson, et al. · 2026

This study investigates how people perceive, negotiate, and develop companionship relationships with AI systems like ChatGPT. We examine the external influences that shape these relationships and the strategies users employ to navigate them.

Crystallizing Schemas with Teleoscope: Thematic Curation of Large Text Corpora

P Bucci, L Foord-Kelcey, PYK Lee, A Singh, I Beschastnikh · 2024

Teleoscope is a tool for thematic curation and sensemaking over large text corpora. It supports researchers in crystallizing analytical schemas through interactive exploration and organization of textual data at scale.
